# Session

Matchmaking in the Menu: the Quick Play search over the Lobby list of the Matchmaking Pool, hosting a Lobby (the Party Session itself, or a fresh Session for a solo Player), merging into an older Lobby, starting the Match for everyone, and leaving back to the Menu (GDD 3.2–3.3, ADR-0016, ADR-0019). No UI; `Meta/Lobby` draws it. Spec: `.scratch/party/spec.md` ("Quick Play search"), ticket `.scratch/party/issues/02-quick-play-search.md`.

## Entry points

- `Matchmaker` — MenuScope entry point (`ITickable`). `Search()`: a solo Player searches at once; a Party Leader whose Party `CanStart` asks the Party to search (`PartyService.StartSearch`), and every member's `Matchmaker` follows the Party's `IsSearching` flag. The searcher (solo Player or Leader) joins the `SessionListFeed`, reads the open Lobbies of its pool and tries `LobbyPick.Candidates` in order (a failed join tries the next one); a Party moves together: the Leader sends `PartyService.MoveTo(name)`, waits `MoveGraceSeconds` for the RPC to leave, and every member leaves the Party Session and joins the target by name. When nothing fits, the searcher hosts a Lobby: the Party Session opens itself (`NetworkSession.OpenAsLobby`, visible and open, with `start`/`opened` properties), a solo Player hosts a new visible Session; the Host spawns the `LobbyLink` and starts the Match when `LobbyRules.ShouldStart` (search time over or full). While hosting only its own Party it keeps reading the list and moves into an older fitting Lobby (`LobbyPick.TryMergeTarget`). `StopSearch()` (any member): in the Party Session it asks the Host (`PartyService.StopSearch`: Party back to hidden, that member Not Ready, guests disconnected); in another Lobby it sends `LobbyLink.RequestPartyStop(code)` so every member of that Party leaves and returns to the Party; a solo Player just leaves. Starting a Match writes `MatchmakingResult(players, MaxSlots, QuickPlay)`, closes the Party link, despawns the `LobbyLink`, closes and hides the Session, and loads the Match scene through Fusion. State for the UI: `Stage` (`Idle`, `Searching`, `Starting`), `SearchSeconds` (local stopwatch since the press, or since a member saw the Party search), `CanSearch`, `CanStopSearch`, `MaxSlots`, `Failure` ("Не удалось подключиться", "Связь с хостом потеряна"; a new `Matchmaker` starts with the notice `SessionExit` kept), `Changed`.
- `Network/LobbyLink` — networked object every Lobby Host spawns (`MatchmakingConfig.LobbyLinkPrefab`, `Prefabs/LobbyLink.prefab`); its only job is `RequestPartyStop(code)`, an RPC to everyone in the Lobby so a Party that moved into a stranger's Lobby can stop together. `Network/LobbyLinks` is its MenuScope registry (`Spawn`, `Despawn`, `PartyStopped`).
- `LobbyPick` (`Simulation/`) — the pure pick and merge rules over `LobbyListing` (name, Players, capacity, start and open time in Unix ms): candidates have free Slots ≥ Party size and ≥ `MinSecondsBeforeStart` (3 s) before their start, ordered by most Players, then soonest start, then name; `TryMergeTarget` picks the best candidate older than the own Lobby (earlier `opened`, ties by name, so of two Lobbies exactly one moves) and only while the own Lobby holds no one but its Party.
- `LobbyRules` (`Simulation/`) — `ShouldStart` (search time over or Session full), `MatchmakingResultFor(players, maxSlots, mode)` (clamps to 1..MaxSlots), `StartsAt(openedAt, searchSeconds)`, `SecondsLeft` (Host's own clock only).
- `SessionExit` — root entry point. `LeaveToMenu()` (or `LeaveToMenu(notice)`, which the Menu then shows through `TakeNotice()`) shuts the Session down and loads the Menu (Hud's "В меню" reaches it through Bootstrap's `MatchExitAdapter`); when a Session is lost while the Match scene is active (the Host left, or the Host disconnected a late Player), it loads the Menu too with the notice `SessionLossNotice` picks from the last `MatchStanding` the Match scene reported through `ObserveMatch` (Bootstrap's `MatchStandingFeed` writes it every frame): "Хост вышел, матч прерван" while the Match is underway (WaitingForPlayers to Playing; Tournament's `MatchReporter` votes it Interrupted when it was Playing), no notice once Results show, "Связь с хостом потеряна" when no running Match was seen. Clears the `MatchmakingResultStore` and the standing.
- `SessionLossNotice` (`Simulation/`) — `For(MatchStanding)`: the notice a lost Session leaves for the Menu (`NotStarted`, `Underway`, `Finished`).
- `MatchmakingConfig` — search seconds (10), max Slots (6), min seconds before a Lobby's start (3), list wait (3 s for the first list), merge check interval (1 s), move grace (0.3 s), `LobbyLink` prefab; asset `_Project/Configs/MatchmakingConfig.asset`.
- `MatchmakingPool`, `LobbyProperties`, `SessionListFeed` (Infrastructure) — the pool name; the Lobby property keys (`pool`, `start`, `opened`, Unix ms as strings) that every Lobby-capable Session declares at creation, because only properties present at creation reach Photon's lobby list; the second, lobby-only `NetworkRunner` that reads the session list while the main runner is inside a Session.

## Rules worth knowing

- Only the Lobby Host's clock decides the start; `start` is published for the 3 s rule of other searchers, never for display. The search screen shows a local stopwatch.
- A Party Session that opened as a Lobby keeps its Party: strangers who join are guests (`PartyJoinOutcome.Guest`), not members; stopping the search disconnects them, and their own `Matchmaker` searches again (a solo guest whose Lobby vanishes restarts the search, keeping the stopwatch).
- A Party whose move fails (the target filled in the meantime) returns to its Party Session instead of trying the next candidate, because members already left; members who did get in stay there. A lost Lobby takes a Party back to its Party, not searching.
- The Menu scope being destroyed by the networked scene load must not leave the Session, so `Matchmaker.Dispose` only unsubscribes and cancels; the `SessionListFeed` is MenuScope-owned and shuts its runner down on dispose.
- The asmdef sets `allowUnsafeCode`: `LobbyLink` declares RPCs, and a weaved RPC calls Fusion internals that Mono lets only an unsafe-compiled assembly reach (`MethodAccessException` otherwise; `Infrastructure/Tests/NetworkWeavingTests` guards it).
