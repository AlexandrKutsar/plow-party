# Snow

The Snow Grid: Snow lying on the Arena, scraped by every Vehicle's Blade, regrowing slowly, re-covered by Blizzard waves, and stacked into Snow Piles by a Spill (GDD 4.1, 4.3, 4.4). Rules live in `Simulation/` as deterministic C#; the Host steps them once per Fusion tick. Decisions: ADR-0012, ADR-0015 (surface rendering). Specs: `.scratch/snow/spec.md`, `.scratch/snow-polish/spec.md`.

## Entry points

- `SnowGrid` — the single test seam. Built from `SnowSettings`, a `VehicleArena` (Cells inside `VehicleArena.Contains` are masked), an optional `ISnowFreeArea`, and a seed. `Scrape(blade, room)` returns the Depth steps taken, at most `room`; `IsOverPile(blade)` and `SpeedMultiplierUnder(blade)` read whether the Blade touches a Snow Pile; `Spill(point, steps)` returns the steps that landed; `Tick(elapsedPlayingTime)` applies Regrowth and Blizzards due by then; `Reset(seed)` starts a new Match: every snowfall Cell back to `FullDepth`, Snow Piles gone, Regrowth schedules and Blizzard progress cleared, the clock back to 0, and a new seed; `GetDepth(x, y)` or `GetDepth(cellIndex)` (row-major) reads a Cell; `WordCount` / `GetWord` / `SetWord` expose the packed form for sync.
- `SnowBlade` — the oriented strip a Blade scrapes; `SnowBlade.Ahead(position, forward, settings)` places it `BladeForwardOffset` in front of a Vehicle.
- `IScrapeLimit` — Snow's capacity seam: `LimitFor(vehicle)` is how many steps that Vehicle's Blade may still take. Bucket implements it, so Snow never references Bucket. The driver passes it to `Scrape` as `room`; the View shares one frame's limit across all pre-clear samples, so a full Bucket shows no pre-cleared track.
- `ISnowFreeArea` — Snow's Snow-Free Area seam: `Contains(point)`. DropOff implements it with a circle around the Drop-Off Zone, so Snow never references DropOff. Queried once per Cell centre when the grid is built.
- `ISnowClock` — Snow's Match seam: `IsPlaying`, `PlayingElapsed`, and the Host-side `MatchRestarted` event. Match's `MatchDriver` implements it, so Snow never references Match (Match → DropOff → Snow).
- `SnowSettings` — tunable numbers, filled from `SnowConfig`.

## Rules worth knowing

- Cells are `CellSize` squares over the `Origin` + `Size` rectangle (Vector2 x = world x, y = world z, as in Vehicle). Depth is 4 bits: 0 cleared, `FullDepth` (3) fully covered, up to `SnowGrid.MaxDepth` (15) as a Snow Pile. Eight Cells pack into one `int`, row-major from Cell (0, 0).
- A Cell whose centre lies inside an Arena box or circle is masked: Depth 0 forever, never scraped, regrown, blizzarded, or piled on.
- A Cell whose centre lies in the `ISnowFreeArea` is snow-free: Depth 0 at start, skipped by Regrowth, Blizzard fronts, and Blizzard Pile placement, but scraped and Spilled on like any other Cell, so Snow Piles land and are collected there. An Obstacle mask wins over snow-free.
- `Scrape` visits Cells under the Blade row by row and stops once `room` steps are taken, so a partial scrape is deterministic. The Blade (0.5 m deep, one Cell) covers one row of Cells at a time.
- Snow Piles are heavy. One `Scrape` takes at most `PileStepsPerScrape` steps from above `FullDepth`, shared by every Cell under the Blade; the driver scrapes once per tick, so at 64 Hz the default 1 is 64 Pile steps per second, about 2.3 s for a 150-step Pile. Snow under a Pile is reachable only once that Cell's Pile steps are gone; plain Cells beside a Pile are not capped. A Blade that moves on keeps only what it took, so a fast pass skims a Pile and leaves the rest. `SpeedMultiplierUnder` is `1 − PileSpeedPenalty` while any Cell under the Blade is deeper than `FullDepth`.
- `Spill` fills the Cell under the point to `MaxDepth`, then square rings around it, each ring row by row, skipping masked Cells.
- Regrowth is age-based, with no randomness (off when `RegrowthStepInterval` is 0). Every snowfall Cell keeps a next Regrowth time, host-only and not networked. `Scrape` taking at least one step and leaving a Cell below `FullDepth` sets it to `now + RegrowthDelay`, `now` being the latest time `Tick` reached (the driver scrapes before ticking, so one tick earlier than the scrape's own tick); a Blade passing over an already-empty Cell, or with no room, leaves the schedule alone; `Tick(t)` then adds one step per `RegrowthStepInterval` while that time is ≤ t, up to `FullDepth`. A track therefore refills oldest end first and fades as a tail; scraping a regrowing Cell again restarts its delay. The schedule depends only on elapsed time, not on how ticks slice it; time going backwards is ignored. Untouched full Cells and Snow Piles have no schedule. Masked and snow-free Cells never regrow, even after a Pile on them is scraped away. A Blizzard only raises Cells, so it never starts a schedule; a stale time left on a Cell it filled does nothing when due (the Cell is already full) and is replaced if the Cell is lowered first.
- Blizzard: a wave starts at each `BlizzardTimes` entry and sweeps the grid in `BlizzardDuration` (0 fills at once) from one of four `BlizzardSide`s, picked by hashing (seed, wave). Cells the front passes are raised to `FullDepth`; a Cell cleared behind the front stays cleared until the next wave; Snow Piles are untouched; a wave missed entirely completes in one `Tick`. Each wave also leaves `BlizzardPilesPerWave` Snow Piles of `BlizzardPileSteps` each, dropped when the front reaches their Cell; the Cells come from hashing (seed, wave, pile), moved to the next Cell that is neither masked nor snow-free (GDD 4.4), which keeps them out of the Snow-Free Area around the Drop-Off Zone.
- Snow speaks in Depth steps, never in Load; Bucket owns the conversion.

## Network and view

- `SnowGridDriver` — scene `NetworkObject` in `Match.unity`. Mirrors the grid into `[Networked, Capacity(1024)] NetworkArray<int>` (`MaxWords`; a 40 × 40 m Arena at 0.5 m Cells needs 800); Fusion's delta compression sends only changed words. Spawning a grid that needs more throws with the grid size and both word counts. On the Host each tick, for every registered Vehicle in Slot order: build its Blade, write the Vehicle's `SnowPile` speed factor from `SpeedMultiplierUnder`, scrape; then, only while `ISnowClock.IsPlaying`, `Tick` with `ISnowClock.PlayingElapsed`; copy words, copy the plowing flags, and raise `Scraped` (`VehicleScrape`: Vehicle, steps) for each non-zero result, so subscribers see the finished tick.
- Plowing a Pile: `IsPlowingPile(vehicle)` reads `[Networked] NetworkArray<NetworkBool>` indexed by Slot on every peer. A Slot is plowing in a tick when its Blade was over a Pile Cell before the scrape and took at least one step, so a full Bucket pushing into a Pile is slowed but does not plow. CameraRig's `CameraPileShake` reads it for the local Vehicle; the View uses it for the snow burst.
- `Spill(point, steps)` is Host-only and ignored before spawn. Clients only read words through `CopyWordsTo`.
- Seed: a random number the Host picks at spawn, not networked and not configured; it only varies Blizzard sides and Blizzard Pile Cells between Matches. Clients never simulate, so they do not need it; client prediction would network it.
- Regrowth times and Blizzard progress live only in the Host's `SnowGrid`, outside `[Networked]` state. Fine without host migration (ADR-0012).
- Match clock: Regrowth and Blizzards advance only during Playing, on the Match's own `PlayingElapsed`, so the 45 / 90 / 135 s waves line up with Hud's Blizzard Announcement. On `MatchRestarted` the Host calls `SnowGrid.Reset` with a fresh seed and copies the words, so every Match starts on a full, pile-free grid with new Blizzard sides.
- `SnowGridView` — draws the grid as a displaced surface (ADR-0015). At first frame it builds a grid mesh of `VerticesPerCell` vertices per Cell edge (`SnowSurfaceMesh`) into the `Model`'s `MeshFilter`, fits the `Model` to the grid rectangle (identity rotation, scale = grid size), and hands `SH_SnowSurface` a height texture through a property block. `SnowHeightField` owns that R8 texture, one texel per Cell holding height / (`SnowHeight` + `PileHeight`); Depth maps to metres piecewise linearly (`SnowHeight` at `FullDepth`, plus `PileHeight` at `MaxDepth`). Only Cells of words that changed get a new target; each shown height eases toward it exponentially (`SurfaceLowerTime` when falling, `SurfaceRaiseTime` when rising), so a confirmed scrape or a Regrowth step never pops, and the texture uploads only on frames where a texel moved. The shader smooths the field with a cubic B-spline in the vertex stage, so no Cell grid shows. Cosmetic pre-clear: the local Player's Blade is sampled at 60 Hz and every sample from the last `PreClearDuration` seconds is scraped on the View's own copy, so the track appears before the Host confirms. Layout comes from `SnowConfig`; the driver only supplies words. The View needs no `ISnowFreeArea`: it overwrites its copy with the Host's words every frame and pre-clear only lowers Depth.
- Snow burst: `SnowGridView`'s optional `Pile Burst` field takes `Art/VFX/SnowBurst/FX_SnowBurst.prefab`; `SnowPileBursts` instantiates one per Slot under the view and keeps it at the Vehicle's rendered Blade, emitting while `IsPlowingPile`. Left empty, no burst is shown.
- `SnowConfig` — the ScriptableObject behind `SnowSettings` plus the View's numbers (pre-clear, mesh density, heights, easing); asset `_Project/Configs/SnowConfig.asset`, registered in `RootLifetimeScope`.
- Scene object `SnowGrid`: `NetworkObject`, `SnowGridDriver` (arena root = `Arena`), `SnowGridView` (`Pile Burst` = `FX_SnowBurst`), and the nested visual prefab `Art/Environment/SnowGrid/V_SnowGrid.prefab` as `Model` (a `MeshFilter` whose mesh the View replaces, and a renderer with `M_SnowGrid` on `SH_SnowSurface`).

## Depends on

Simulation: `UnityEngine` math and Vehicle's `VehicleArena`. Network and View: Fusion, VContainer, Vehicle (`VehicleRegistry`, `NetworkVehicle` and its `SnowPile` speed factor, `VehicleArenaReader`), and an `IScrapeLimit`, `ISnowFreeArea`, and `ISnowClock` registered in `MatchScope` (all from other modules). CameraRig reads `SnowGridDriver`, so Snow must never reference CameraRig. Vehicle never references Snow. The assembly is in Fusion's `AssembliesToWeave`.
