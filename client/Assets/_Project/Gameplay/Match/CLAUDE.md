# Match

The Match lifecycle inside a Session: WaitingForPlayers → Countdown → Playing → Results (one Match per Session), who takes which Slot, the Match clock, and the placement table (GDD 3.1–3.3). Rules live in `Simulation/` as deterministic C#; the Host steps them once per Fusion tick. Specs: `.scratch/match/spec.md`, `.scratch/bots/spec.md` (WaitingForPlayers and seating), `.scratch/party/spec.md` (quick Bots, no "Play again", Custom Game seating).

## Entry points

- `MatchRules` — phase machine and clock, built from `MatchSettings`: `NextPhase(phase, phaseElapsed, allSlotsFilled)`, `PhaseRemaining`, `PlayingElapsed`, `PlayingRemaining`, static `IsInputLocked(phase)`, and `PhaseElapsed(tick, phaseStartTick, deltaTime)`. Holds no state.
- `WaitingRules` — who fills the Slots while waiting: `PlanSeats(hasMatchmakingResult, matchmakingResult, playersPresent, slotLimit)` → `SeatPlan` (Slot count, expected Players, planned Bots), `ScheduleBotArrivals(count, random)` (sorted times inside the arrival window), `HurryBotArrivals(arrivals, seatedBots, now, random)` (the Bots not seated yet rescheduled into the quick window from `now`), `BotsToSeat(plan, players, bots, waitingElapsed, arrivals)`, `IsWaitingOver(plan, players, bots, waitingElapsed)` (every Slot taken, or a Custom Game at the cap with all its Bots seated), static `AllSlotsFilled`, `HasFreeSlot` and `AllExpectedPlayersSeated`. Holds no state.
- `CustomGameSeats` — `BotSlot(setup, n)` (the Slot of the n-th Bot of a `CustomGameSetup`) and `ReservedColors(setup, seatedSlots)` (picks of Players not seated yet, which Bots may not take).
- `IBotChoices` (`Network/`) — `TryGetChosenDifficulty(slot)`: the Difficulty the Party Leader chose for a Custom Game Bot; `MatchSeating` implements it, Bots read it.
- `MatchPlacementRules.Rank(slots, scores, count, output)` — fills `MatchPlacement`s (Slot, Score, Place) ordered by Score descending, ties by Slot; tied Scores share a place and skip the next (1, 2, 2, 4), like the backend. Allocation-free; returns the rows written.
- `IMatchClock` — read-only clock on every peer: `IsRunning`, `Phase`, `MatchNumber` (always 1 now; Tournament's reporting keys on it), `PhaseRemaining`, `PlayingElapsed`, `PlayingRemaining`.
- `IMatchResults` — the recorded placement table (`PlacementCount`, `GetPlacement(index)`).
- `IMatchAdmission` — `IsLocalPlayerTurnedAway`: whether the Host turned this device's Player away (arrived after Countdown or with every Slot taken).
- `MatchSettings` — durations, the two Bot arrival windows and the fallback Slot count, filled from `MatchConfig`.

## Rules worth knowing

- WaitingForPlayers (cap 15 s) → Countdown 3 s → Playing 180 s → Results, which has no timer and no "Play again": it stays until each Player presses "В меню" (Hud's `IMatchExit`). When the Host leaves, the Session ends for everyone (no host migration) and Meta's `SessionExit` takes the clients out, except on Results: Hud's `ResultsView` keeps the last table on screen after the Session ends, until the Player presses "В меню".
- WaitingForPlayers ends the moment every Slot is taken (Players plus Bots), never on time alone. The cap only applies while an expected Player is missing: then at the cap every free Slot gets a Bot at once, which fills them. The phase remaining time is the cap countdown Hud shows.
- Seat plan: on the Host, `MatchmakingResultStore.TryGet` gives the Players Meta matched (`ExpectedHumans`) and `MaxSlots`, both clamped to the Spawn Points. Without a Matchmaking Result (Match scene started directly, dev quick start) the Host expects the Players already connected (at least itself) in `FallbackSlotCount` (6) Slots. The plan reserves Slots for expected Players; Bots fill the rest gradually at random times inside `BotArrivalStart`–`BotArrivalEnd` (1–8 s) while someone is still loading. The tick every expected Player is seated (at once for a solo Player or the dev quick start), `MatchSeating` hurries the Bots not seated yet to random times `QuickBotArrivalStart`–`QuickBotArrivalEnd` (0.5–2.5 s) from then, once per Match, so nobody waits for nobody. An unexpected extra Player takes a free Slot while there is one, which leaves one Bot fewer.
- The Slot list is fixed when Countdown starts (the backend Roster, ADR-0013). A Player who connects later is refused at the connection request; one who slips through the join is turned away: `MatchSeating` lists them in `TurnedAwayPlayers`, `MatchDriver` publishes the list as networked state, the client reads it through `IMatchAdmission.IsLocalPlayerTurnedAway` and leaves itself, and the Host disconnects them after `TurnAwayGraceSeconds` (5 s) if they are still there; both cases are logged. The refused client's start answers `SessionStartOutcome.Refused` and Meta shows "Матч уже начался" in the Menu (from Quick Play or a Party Code, and from `MatchSceneQuickStart`, which leaves to the Menu); a turned-away client gets the same notice through Meta's `SessionExit` (Bootstrap's `MatchStandingFeed` reports `MatchStanding.TurnedAway`).
- Custom Game (the Matchmaking Result carries a `CustomGameSetup`): the plan has exactly the setup's Slots (members and Leader's Bots, empty Slots dropped) and `ReplacesMissingPlayers` is false. Each Player takes the Slot of their `PlayerRef` with their picked Critter and colour (passed as `preferred` to `ParticipantColors.Assign`); a Player who is not in the setup is turned away; the n-th Bot takes the n-th Bot Slot, avoiding the colours of Players not seated yet. Bots arrive on the usual schedule and are hurried once every member is in. At the 15 s cap with a member missing, the remaining Bots are seated, no Bot replaces the member, and Countdown starts with the empty Slot.
- A Player who leaves during a Match leaves the Slot empty for the rest of it.
- The networked state is the phase and the tick it started; every peer derives the clock from its own tick, so nothing is written per tick except the input lock. A client's tick is predicted ahead of the Host, so near a transition its clock can read a fraction of a second past the phase end until the new phase arrives; views clamp (Countdown never shows 0).
- Input is locked outside Playing (WaitingForPlayers included) through Vehicle's `IsImmobilised` Modifier, written by the Host every tick for every registered Vehicle. `MatchDriver` carries `[DefaultExecutionOrder(-100)]` so it runs before `VehicleWorldDriver` and the lock applies on the very tick Playing ends. Match is that Modifier's only owner until Freeze exists.
- Placements are recorded once, on entering Results, from DropOff's `IScoreReader`, so Score credited by a Delivery still unloading after the end does not change the table.

## Network

- `MatchDriver` — scene `NetworkObject` (`Prefabs/MatchDriver.prefab`), implements both interfaces and Snow's `ISnowClock` (`IsPlaying`, `PlayingElapsed`); registered in `MatchScope` with `RegisterComponentInHierarchy` as `IMatchClock`, `IMatchResults`, `ISnowClock`, `IMatchAdmission`, and itself. It publishes the turned-away Players (`TurnedAway`, up to 8) every Host tick. The Host plans the seats and enters WaitingForPlayers on spawn (Match number 1); every tick it lets `MatchSeating` seat arrivals and due Bots before stepping the phase.
- `MatchSeating` — host-side entry point in `MatchScope` (also registered as itself for `MatchDriver`). Owns which Slot holds whom: queues `PlayerJoined` arrivals and seats them on the next tick (Nickname from the `ParticipantToken` in the connection token via Participants' rules, fallback "Игрок N"; a random Critter; a random free Participant Color through `ParticipantColors.Assign` with `NoPreference`; Account Id kept host-side), refuses `ConnectRequested` once closed or full, seats Bots (pool Nickname, random free Slot, random free Participant Color) through `ParticipantRoster.Seat` and `VehicleSpawner.Spawn` with no input authority, and vacates a leaving Player's Slot. Logs every seating decision under `[Match]`, the hurry included ("Every expected Player is in after N s").
- `MatchConfig` — the ScriptableObject behind `MatchSettings` plus `TurnAwayGraceSeconds` (5 s); asset `_Project/Configs/MatchConfig.asset`, registered in `RootLifetimeScope`.

## Depends on

Participants (`ParticipantRoster`, `ParticipantProfiles`, `ParticipantsConfig`), DropOff (`IScoreReader`), Snow (`ISnowClock`, implemented here), Vehicle (`VehicleRegistry`, `VehicleSpawner`, `NetworkVehicle`), Infrastructure (`NetworkRunnerEvents`, `MatchmakingResultStore`), Shared (`MatchmakingResult`, `CustomGameSetup`, `BotDifficulty`, `ParticipantToken`), Fusion, VContainer. Hud and Bots depend on Match. Snow cannot reference Match (Match → DropOff → Snow), which is why the clock seam is Snow's interface. The assembly is in Fusion's `AssembliesToWeave`. Submitting the Match Result belongs to Meta. Decision: ADR-0018.
