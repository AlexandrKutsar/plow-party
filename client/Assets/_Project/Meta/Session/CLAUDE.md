# Session

Matchmaking in the Menu: the Quick Play search over the Lobby list of the Matchmaking Pool, hosting a Lobby (the Party Session itself, or a fresh Session for a solo Player), merging into an older Lobby, starting the Match for everyone, and leaving back to the Menu (GDD 3.2–3.3, ADR-0016, ADR-0019). No UI; `Meta/Lobby` draws it. Spec: `.scratch/party/spec.md` ("Quick Play search"), tickets `.scratch/party/issues/02-quick-play-search.md` and `06-custom-game.md`.

## Entry points

- `Matchmaker` — MenuScope entry point (`ITickable`). `Search()`: a solo Player searches at once; a Party Leader whose Party `CanStart` asks the Party to search (`PartyService.StartSearch`), and every member's `Matchmaker` follows the Party's `IsSearching` flag. The searcher (solo Player or Leader) joins the `SessionListFeed`, reads the open Lobbies of its pool and tries `LobbyPick.Candidates` in order (a failed join tries the next one); a Party moves Leader first: the Leader sends `PartyService.MoveTo(name)`, waits `MoveGraceSeconds` for the RPC to leave, leaves the Party Session and joins the candidate; a failed join tries the next candidate. Once in, the Leader calls `LobbyLink.RequestPartyArrived(code)`, and the Lobby Host lists the Party Code in the Lobby's `parties` property (`LobbyParties`, dropped when that Leader leaves). Members leave the Party Session on `MoveRequested`, read the session list until a Lobby lists their Party Code (`LobbyParties.TryFindLobbyOf`, at most `FollowSeconds`) and join that one; a member in a Lobby whose `parties` no longer lists its Party (the Leader left) is called back to the Party. When every candidate fails, the Leader returns to the Party Session and the search ends; members who saw no Leader in `FollowSeconds` return too. When nothing fits, the searcher hosts a Lobby: the Party Session opens itself (`NetworkSession.OpenAsLobby`, visible and open, with `start`/`opened` properties), a solo Player hosts a new visible Session; the Host spawns the `LobbyLink` and starts the Match when `LobbyRules.ShouldStart` (search time over or full). While hosting only its own Party it keeps reading the list and moves into an older fitting Lobby (`LobbyPick.TryMergeTarget`). When the mode is Custom Game (`IsCustomGame`, the Party's or the solo Player's mode), `Search()` starts the Match at once instead: a Party Leader loads it from the Party Session, a solo Player first hosts a hidden one-Player Session; the Matchmaking Result is `new MatchmakingResult(PartyService.CustomGameSetupFor(local PlayerRef))` and the start log is "Custom Game starts with N Player(s) and M Bot(s)". `StopSearch()` (any member): in the Party Session it asks the Host (`PartyService.StopSearch`: Party back to hidden, that member Not Ready, guests disconnected); in another Lobby it sends `LobbyLink.RequestPartyStop(code)` so every member of that Party leaves and returns to the Party; a solo Player just leaves. Starting a Match writes `MatchmakingResult(players, MaxSlots, QuickPlay)`, closes the Party link, despawns the `LobbyLink`, closes and hides the Session, and loads the Match scene through Fusion. State for the UI: `Stage` (`Idle`, `Searching`, `Starting`), `SearchSeconds` (local stopwatch since the press, or since a member saw the Party search), `CanSearch`, `CanStopSearch`, `MaxSlots`, `Failure` (`SessionNotice`: `ConnectFailed`, `ConnectionLost`; a new `Matchmaker` starts with the notice `SessionExit` kept; `Meta/Lobby`'s `LobbyText` words it), `Changed`.
- `Network/LobbyLink` — networked object every Lobby Host spawns (`MatchmakingConfig.LobbyLinkPrefab`, `Prefabs/LobbyLink.prefab`): `RequestPartyStop(code)`, an RPC to everyone in the Lobby so a Party that moved into a stranger's Lobby can stop together; `RequestPartyArrived(code)`, a Party Leader's RPC to the Lobby Host that it is in; the Host's `PlayerLeft`. `Network/LobbyLinks` is its MenuScope registry (`Spawn`, `Despawn`, `PartyStopped`, `PartyArrived`, `PlayerLeft`).
- `LobbyParties` (`Simulation/`) — the `parties` property: `Format(codes)`, `Contains(parties, code)`, `TryFindLobbyOf(listings, code)` (the Lobby a Party's Leader is in).
- `LobbyPick` (`Simulation/`) — the pure pick and merge rules over `LobbyListing` (name, Players, capacity, start and open time in Unix ms, `parties`): candidates have free Slots ≥ Party size and ≥ `MinSecondsBeforeStart` (3 s) before their start, ordered by most Players, then soonest start, then name; `TryMergeTarget` picks the best candidate older than the own Lobby (earlier `opened`, ties by name, so of two Lobbies exactly one moves) and only while the own Lobby holds no one but its Party.
- `LobbyRules` (`Simulation/`) — `ShouldStart` (search time over or Session full), `MatchmakingResultFor(players, maxSlots, mode)` (clamps to 1..MaxSlots), `StartsAt(openedAt, searchSeconds)`, `SecondsLeft` (Host's own clock only).
- `SessionExit` — root entry point. `LeaveToMenu()` (or `LeaveToMenu(SessionNotice)`, which the Menu then shows through `TakeNotice()`) shuts the Session down and loads the Menu (Hud's "В меню" reaches it through Bootstrap's `MatchExitAdapter`). `ObserveMatch(MatchStanding)` gets the Match's standing every frame from Bootstrap's `MatchStandingFeed`; `TurnedAway` (the Host turned this late Player away) leaves at once with `MatchStarted` ("Матч уже начался"). When a Session is lost while the Match scene is active, the last standing decides (`SessionLossNotice`): on Results the Player stays on the frozen table without a notice until "В меню"; otherwise it loads the Menu with `HostLeft` ("Хост вышел, матч прерван") when it was Playing (Tournament's `MatchReporter` votes it Interrupted), `ConnectionLost` ("Связь с хостом потеряна") before Playing, `MatchStarted` when turned away. Clears the `MatchmakingResultStore` and the standing.
- `SessionLossNotice` (`Simulation/`) — `For(MatchStanding)`: the `SessionNotice` a lost Session leaves for the Menu; `KeepsResults(MatchStanding)`: whether the Player stays on Results. Standings: `BeforePlaying`, `Playing`, `Finished`, `TurnedAway`.
- `MatchmakingConfig` — search seconds (10), max Slots (6), min seconds before a Lobby's start (3), list wait (3 s for the first list), merge check interval (1 s), move grace (0.3 s), follow time (8 s: how long members wait for their Leader's Lobby), `LobbyLink` prefab; asset `_Project/Configs/MatchmakingConfig.asset`.
- `MatchmakingPool`, `LobbyProperties`, `SessionListFeed` (Infrastructure) — the pool name; the Lobby property keys (`pool`, `start`, `opened` as Unix ms strings, `parties`) that every Lobby-capable Session declares at creation, because only properties present at creation reach Photon's lobby list; the second, lobby-only `NetworkRunner` that reads the session list while the main runner is inside a Session.

## Rules worth knowing

- Only the Lobby Host's clock decides the start; `start` is published for the 3 s rule of other searchers, never for display. The search screen shows a local stopwatch.
- A Party Session that opened as a Lobby keeps its Party: strangers who join are guests (`PartyJoinOutcome.Guest`), not members; stopping the search disconnects them, and their own `Matchmaker` searches again (a solo guest whose Lobby vanishes restarts the search, keeping the stopwatch).
- A Party moves Leader first, so a failed join costs only the Leader's attempt and the next candidate is tried; members join only a Lobby that lists their Party Code, and leave it when the code disappears. A lost Lobby takes a Party back to its Party, not searching.
- The Menu scope being destroyed by the networked scene load must not leave the Session, so `Matchmaker.Dispose` only unsubscribes and cancels; the `SessionListFeed` is MenuScope-owned and shuts its runner down on dispose.
- The asmdef sets `allowUnsafeCode`: `LobbyLink` declares RPCs, and a weaved RPC calls Fusion internals that Mono lets only an unsafe-compiled assembly reach (`MethodAccessException` otherwise; `Infrastructure/Tests/NetworkWeavingTests` guards it).
